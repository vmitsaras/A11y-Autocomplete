Deterministic playground

Put every interaction state within reach.

Choose a fixture, interact with the field, and inspect the live DOM state. Async work waits for you to resolve or reject it—there are no requests or timing races.

Scenario and lifecycle controls

Pending work cancels when focus leaves the field. Use the pointer controls without moving focus, or press Alt+Shift+R to resolve, E to reject, and T to retry.

Manual announcement check

  1. With a screen reader running, type in Local and confirm the result count, active option, selection, and clearing sequence.
  2. In Async, type a query, then resolve and reject requests; confirm loading, results, and error announcements occur once.
  3. In Strict, submit “Unknown”; confirm focus returns to the field and the invalid message is associated with it.
